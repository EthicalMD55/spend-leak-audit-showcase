Trust model

The system is designed to keep every step transparent and to stop before producing an actionable recommendation. Potential savings are upper-bound estimates that only become recommendations after a human confirms the underlying data signal.

The system does not claim guaranteed savings or automatically cancel services.
Potential savings require human verification. Every quantified figure is an upper bound derived from deterministic rules against the supplied data — not a guaranteed outcome. Actual results depend on seat usage, contract terms, shared accounts, cancellation policies, and other factors confirmed only by a human reviewer.
