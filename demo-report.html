8. Methodology

The audit is run by the Spend Leak Audit Engine v0.2, a deterministic, local-only script (Python 3 stdlib) reading three CSV files: vendor spend, usage, and employee/license data.

  • Deterministic rules. No LLMs, no external APIs, no network calls. Identical inputs + reference date produce byte-identical output (verified across 3 runs).
  • Labels. POTENTIAL SAVING = a quantified annual amount that could be avoided if a human confirms it (capped at vendor annual cost, never negative, never double-counted). NEEDS HUMAN VERIFICATION = a flag carrying potential_savings == 0.0 by invariant.
  • Savings integrity (enforced, not claimed). Quantified total sums POTENTIAL SAVING findings only; NHVM excluded. Total floored at 0 and capped at total spend. No per-vendor POTENTIAL SAVING exceeds that vendor's annual cost.
  • Source traceability. Every finding exposes its source file, source row, and relevant cost/seat fields.
  • Human confirmation required. Heuristics (2x seat ratio; full-annual-cost for zero usage) are upper bounds with unknown false-positive rates. Reserved seats, shared mailboxes, contractors, seasonal use, negotiated discounts, bundled licences, cancellation fees, and contract terms can all change the result.

9. Important Disclaimer

This report is decision-support only. It is based solely on the data supplied to the audit engine for this engagement.
  • No guarantees. Savings figures are estimates from deterministic rules -- NOT guaranteed; actual results will differ.
  • Human verification required. Every potential saving requires confirmation that the resource is genuinely spare and that cancellation/downgrade is appropriate.
  • Factors that may change results. Contract terms, business requirements, reserved seats, shared accounts, seasonal use, cancellation fees, negotiated discounts, bundled licences, currency conversion, and headcount growth may all materially change the actual saving.
  • No advice. This is not accounting, tax, legal, or financial advice. Do not rely on these figures without independent review.
  • Data quality. Totals exclude non-USD rows, rows with unparseable cost data, and rows with missing usage data. Completing the source data may change every figure.
  • Scope. The engine audits the supplied CSV files only; it does not pull live data from vendor APIs or HRIS systems.
